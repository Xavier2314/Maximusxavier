# Build the ad bot, but gate it carefully

**Verdict: yes, this is feasible, with conditions.** Meta lets third-party apps create campaigns, ad sets, creatives and ads inside a customer's own ad account through the Marketing API (current version v26.0, released 29 July 2026) ([Meta changelog](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/graph-api/changelog/version26.0/)). Your Next.js/Supabase stack can handle the orchestration. The real gates are not technical. First, Meta must approve your app for "Advanced Access" to the `ads_management` permission, which requires App Review plus Business Verification of your company ([unified.to](https://unified.to/blog/how_to_get_your_meta_ads_api_key)). Second, Nigerian sellers often cannot pay Meta because their naira cards are blocked for international use ([Grey](https://grey.co/blog/pay-for-facebook-ads-in-nigeria)). Third, health and supplement ads are the highest-risk category on Meta, and a bot that mass-produces them can get your users' accounts, and eventually your app, restricted ([Meta Advertising Standards](https://transparency.meta.com/policies/ad-standards/)). My recommendation is to build a **"draft, check, approve, publish, monitor" assistant** in which the seller pays Meta directly and approves every ad. Do not build a fully autonomous bot that holds users' ad money. **Important caveat:** the research environment could not open any Meta page, so everything below comes from search-result summaries and third-party write-ups. Section 7 lists what must be re-checked against Meta's live documentation before you commit money to the build.

## What "possible" means: five yes/no answers

| Question | Answer | Confidence |
|---|---|---|
| Can an app build and publish ads in a user's own Facebook ad account? | Yes, via the Marketing API | Fairly solid (Meta docs surfaced in search) |
| Can you do this for real customers without Meta's approval? | No. Only people with a role on your app (you, testers) work without it | Solid in direction, unverified in detail |
| Can the user pay the ad spend themselves? | Yes, that is the normal model. Meta bills the user's own account | Solid |
| Can the bot make images and videos that sell? | Yes for images and template videos at low cost; fully AI-generated videos of products are unreliable | Mixed (see section 5) |
| Can it be fully hands-off for health products? | Not safely. Human approval per ad is the right default | Strong inference |

## What Meta requires: approval, permissions and tiers

**Your app needs Advanced Access, which means App Review and Business Verification.** Standard access only works for ad accounts belonging to people who hold a role on your app. To serve strangers in Nigeria, you must verify your own company as a business and pass App Review with screencasts of the full login flow and a working ads demo showing metrics such as impressions, spend and clicks ([Permissions Reference](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/permissions), [overads.io](https://overads.io/blog/read-only-ad-access)). Business Verification is reported to take from a few business days up to weeks ([360dialog](https://docs.360dialog.com/partner/get-started/tech-provider-program/become-a-meta-tech-provider), [singhamandeep.com](https://singhamandeep.com/facebook-ads-api-permission-app-review/)). Whether a Nigeria-registered company can complete this, and with which documents, was **not found**; check this first, because it blocks everything else.

**The realistic permission list** (my inference from Meta's permission definitions) is `ads_management` (create and manage ads), `ads_read` (reporting), `business_management`, `pages_show_list`, `pages_read_engagement` and `pages_manage_ads` ([Permissions Reference](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/permissions)). Request only what you demonstrate: one practitioner reported `ads_read` and `business_management` approved while `ads_management` was declined, and another had a bundled all-scopes submission rejected twice ([singhamandeep.com case study](https://singhamandeep.com/ads_read-permission-approved/)). These are anecdotes from people selling services, so treat them as a warning, not a statistic.

**Tiers were renamed in May 2026.** Standard became "Limited" and Advanced became "Full". The threshold to upgrade fell to 500 Marketing API calls in 15 days with an error rate below 15% ([Meta blog](https://developers.meta.com/blog/updates-to-ads-management-standard-access-feature/)). Full tier allows unlimited ad accounts and lighter rate limiting ([Rate limiting doc](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/ads-commerce/marketing-api/overview/rate-limiting)). Meta is also reported to be dropping "Access Verification" from Tech Provider onboarding, while Business Verification and App Review remain ([Social Samosa](https://www.socialsamosa.com/news-2/meta-removes-access-verification-tech-provider-app-onboarding-12667685)).

**Login and tokens.** Meta positions Facebook Login for Business as the preferred sign-in for apps acting on clients' assets; tokens are limited to the assets the client chose in the login flow, and the client can revoke access at any time ([Meta docs](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/facebook-login/facebook-login-for-business)). System-user tokens can be non-expiring or last 60 days ([Meta docs](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/business-management-apis/system-users/install-apps-and-generate-tokens/)). Plan a "reconnect Facebook" screen, because tokens die when users change passwords, lose Page roles or remove your app.

**Things the user still has to do in Meta's own screens** (general knowledge, unverified here): have a Facebook Page, an ad account, a payment method, and accept Meta's terms. Your onboarding should check these through the API and tell the seller exactly what is missing.

**The structure you must build has changed.** Old "Advantage+ Shopping" campaign creation through the API is closed. Meta blocked new creation from v24.0 (October 2025) and create/update from v25.0 (February 2026) ([Meta/PPC Land](https://ppc.land/meta-deprecates-legacy-campaign-apis-for-advantage-structure/)). The replacement is a campaign with Advantage+ audience, campaign-level budget and Advantage+ placements switched on. Dates differ across secondary sources, so pin your build to v25 or v26 and test against the live API. Meta publishes new versions about every six months, and v24 was due to expire on 6 October 2026 ([versions page](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/graph-api/changelog/versions)).

## Who pays whom: payment in Nigeria

**Recommended model: the seller pays Meta directly and pays you a separate subscription.** Meta bills the owner of the ad account. Search summaries of Meta's partner documentation describe a separate "partner pays Meta" model, but the notes could not confirm whether it is open to a new Nigerian company ([Meta onboarding doc](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-sdk/common-scenarios/onboard-at-scale)). Established ad tools price as a subscription scaled by managed spend, and the spend stays in the user's Meta billing. Examples are Birch/Revealbot and Madgicx ([Superscale](https://superscale.ai/alternatives/revealbot/pricing), [Toolradar](https://toolradar.com/tools/madgicx/pricing)). That model also keeps you out of the business of holding customers' money.

**Why not collect the ad budget yourself.** In that reseller model you become the advertiser of record. You would carry the risk of chargebacks, unpaid bills and policy violations, and one bad ad could disable an account shared by many sellers. Holding customer funds may also be a regulated activity in Nigeria; the notes found a December 2023 NIBSS directive about switches and payment companies not being allowed to hold customer funds, with Flutterwave saying it was unaffected ([BusinessDay](https://businessday.ng/news/article/banks-ordered-to-cut-off-flutterwave-remita-others-from-transfer-services/)). Whether ad-budget collection needs a licence is an inference that **needs a Nigerian lawyer**. There is also a tax drawback: Meta adds VAT, and a user who pays through you cannot reclaim input VAT on an invoice issued to you. Agency practitioners likewise advise that clients pay Meta directly ([AdManage](https://admanage.ai/blog/how-to-run-facebook-ads-for-clients)). The one commercial counter-example is Zeely, which reportedly charges a 12% fee on ad spend, so a percentage fee is possible later ([Creatify](https://creatify.ai/blog/zeely-pricing-(2026)-plans-credits-and-what-you-ll-actually-pay)). Keep any percentage fee billed separately from Meta's invoice.

**The Nigerian card problem is real and is your biggest onboarding drop-off risk.** Meta processes payments through an international gateway even for naira billing, and many Nigerian banks block international use of naira cards or cap it at roughly $20-50 per month ([Grey](https://grey.co/blog/pay-for-facebook-ads-in-nigeria), [Spendfigo](https://spendfigo.com/blog/why-my-nigerian-visa-card-keeps-getting-declined-on-meta-ads-and-what-actually-f)). The usual workaround is a dollar virtual card or domiciliary card. Bank limits were reported to be loosening in 2026, but the reports conflict: GTBank's quarterly limit appears as $20,000 in one article and $40,000 in another ([IT Edge News](https://www.itedgenews.africa/gtbank-sets-20000-quarterly-spending-limit-on-naira-debit-cards-as-nigerian-lenders-ramp-up-international-payments/)). Nearly all sources sell cards, so they are biased. A failed charge can also restrict the ad account until paid, with a reported 24-48 hour grace period, and payment-related disablements can be hard to reverse ([Adnova](https://www.adnova.ai/blog/facebook-ad-account-disabled)). So build a guided "set up your dollar card" step, a payment-failure alert, and a clear note on spending limits.

**Taxes sit on top of the budget the seller sets.** Reported rates, all dated and needing a current check, are Nigeria 7.5% VAT ([TheCable](https://www.thecable.ng/?p=637748)), Kenya 16% VAT ([TechCabal](https://techcabal.com/2022/11/03/techcabal-daily-kenyans-pay-facebook-ads/)) and Ghana 15% VAT plus levies ([Liquid Design summary](https://element.liquiddesign.co.uk/ghana/237964-more-taxes-facebook-start-charging-ghanaians-15-vat-ads)). Label every in-app budget "before tax". Collect your own subscription through Paystack or Flutterwave in naira; that is background knowledge, as the notes did not verify fee schedules or Stripe availability.

## Health and supplement ads: the compliance minefield

**Meta's rules are strict, and an AI that writes freely will break them.** Meta bans ads that assert or imply knowledge of a person's health or other personal attributes ([Meta: personal attributes](https://transparency.meta.com/policies/ad-standards/objectionable-content/privacy-violations-personal-attributes/)). Dietary, health and weight-loss ads must be targeted to people 18 and over, may not make people feel negatively about their bodies, and face restrictions on before-and-after imagery ([Meta: health and wellness](https://transparency.meta.com/policies/ad-standards/restricted-goods-services/health-wellness)). "Unsafe supplements" are banned, including a non-exhaustive list of anabolic steroids, chitosan, comfrey, DHEA, ephedra and human growth hormone ([Meta: unsafe supplements](https://transparency.meta.com/policies/ad-standards/dangerous-content/unsafe-supplements)). Ads promoting income opportunities must fully describe the business model and must not promote quick compensation for little investment, including multi-level marketing ([Meta: unrealistic outcomes](https://transparency.meta.com/policies/ad-standards/deceptive-content/unrealistic-outcomes)). Many of your sellers sit exactly in this zone. These quotes come from search summaries of Meta's pages, not from reading the pages, so verify the wording.

**Enforcement is automated and punishes the account, not just the ad.** Meta says rejected ads can lead to restrictions on the business account or its assets, and ads can be re-reviewed at any time ([Meta Advertising Standards](https://transparency.meta.com/policies/ad-standards/)). In December 2025, 35 US state attorneys general told Meta its health-ad policies are under-enforced, citing AI-generated before-and-after weight-loss ads ([PA Attorney General](https://www.attorneygeneral.gov/?p=32582)). Expect tightening, not loosening. 2026 reports also link account bans to unattended bursts of automated writes, shared credentials and immediate retries; these are anecdotes, many from vendors selling alternatives ([Porter Metrics](https://portermetrics.com/en/tutorial/meta-ads-mcp-ban/), [Supermetrics](https://supermetrics.com/blog/ai-agent-ad-account-banned)). Meta set no published number of rejections before restriction.

**Nigeria adds a second layer, and it is stricter than Meta's.** NAFDAC's 2019 advertisement regulations say no one may advertise a drug or related product unless it is registered, and ads need the agency's pre-clearance; herbal medicine ads also need registration and approval ([PolicyVault](https://policyvault.africa/policy/national-agency-for-food-and-drug-product-administration-and-control-nafdac-drug-and-related-products-advertisement-regulations-2019/)). Registration alone is not ad approval ([BusinessDay](https://clone.businessday.ng/?p=59262)). Ghana's FDA likewise requires prior vetting of food ads and stressed in June 2025 that a registered product is not automatically approved for advertising ([GhanaWeb](https://www.ghanaweb.com/GhanaHomePage/NewsArchive/Registered-products-not-a-guarantee-for-advertisement-FDA-1986423)). ARCON sued Meta in 2022 over unvetted ads, but the case was struck out in 2024 after ARCON discontinued it, so its vetting claim is untested ([Techpoint](https://techpoint.africa/2024/07/22/court-dismisses-arcons-lawsuit-meta/)). On data, Nigeria's NDPA 2023 and the 2025 GAID replaced the old NDPR and reach anyone targeting Nigerian data subjects ([Mondaq](https://www.mondaq.com/nigeria/privacy-protection/1627428/key-regulatory-updates-under-the-2025-general-application-and-implementation-directive-gaid-issued-by-the-nigeria-data-protection-commission-ndpc)); this matters because your funnels and WhatsApp collect leads. Rules for Kenya, South Africa and Nigerian MLM advertising were **not found**.

**Design the bot so it is hard to break these rules.** The notes propose a pre-publish checklist, which I endorse as the core of the product (my design, not a Meta requirement): block disease, cure, treat and prevent claims; block quantified or guaranteed results; flag second-person condition phrasing such as "Are you struggling with belly fat?"; block earnings claims and recruitment copy; block before-and-after, body close-ups and scale images; require a NAFDAC number per product, plus an advert approval number for drugs and herbals; set health ads to adults only; and pause on any rejection with no auto-resubmit. Add velocity limits (a few new ads per day on a new account), draft-first publishing and per-ad human approval. Warn sellers in plain words that a violation can affect their Page, ad account and sometimes their personal profile. For AI media, never generate fake customers, doctors or testimonials; Meta labels AI-made ads, and mandatory disclosure is documented for political ads, with wider rules on realistic synthetic people reported but unconfirmed ([Social Media Today](https://socialmediatoday.com/news/meta-adds-updated-disclosure-tags-for-ai-generated-ads/824658)). Also, if copy mentions hiring, loans, property or politics, stop and require a human decision, because Meta's special ad categories (housing, employment, financial products, social issues) limit targeting ([Jon Loomer](https://www.jonloomer.com/special-ad-categories-meta-ads/)). No source reviewed this against Africa.

## What ads that sell look like, and what they cost to make

**Creative diversity beats audience tricks.** Meta's retrieval system, Andromeda, narrows the ad pool before the auction, and practitioners read Meta's own posts as saying that creative variety is now the main lever ([Jon Loomer](https://www.jonloomer.com/meta-andromeda/)). Near-duplicate variants with a new hook do not count as diversity. So the bot should produce **3-5 genuinely different concepts** (a problem demo, an objection FAQ, an offer-led static, a founder talking to camera), not 20 reworded copies. The claims that a "60% similarity score" triggers suppression, or that diversity lifts conversions 17%, have no primary source; do not hard-code them ([Chatterbuzz](https://www.chatterbuzzmedia.com/blog/meta-andromeda-creative-targeting/)).

**Format guidance with evidence behind it.** Vertical 9:16 video, with sound, inside the safe zone and with an extra element (a person, text overlay, voiceover or an early hook), showed about 20% lower cost per click and 56% higher click-through rate in an analysis of Meta data reported by Adobe ([Adobe](https://blog.adobe.com/en/publish/2024/11/04/unlock-potential-reels-best-practices-for-creative-success-using-adobe-creative-cloud)). One agency guide says keep the bottom ~35% of a vertical frame free of text and logos, and that ads violating the safe zone had 28% lower click-through ([Tiger Pistol](https://tigerpistol.com/vertical-video-ad-best-practices/)); safe zones may have shifted in 2026, so check Meta's current spec. Hooks should work in the first 2-3 seconds with sound off. Motion's dataset (about $1.29 billion of Meta spend) lists offer-first banners, demos, testimonials, unboxing, before-and-after, listicles and us-versus-them among the best-performing formats, at hit rates of roughly 5-10% ([Motion](https://motionapp.com/library/formats/)); remove before-and-after for health products. Static images are about 46% of Meta ads and scored about 13% better on average in one vendor's 217,000-ad analysis, whose methodology is unverified ([AdKit](https://adkit.so/glossary/static-ad)). Concrete, specific claims with short overlay text and real social proof beat vague, text-heavy images ([Superads](https://www.superads.ai/blog/static-ads-examples)). Turn off Meta's automatic text rewriting and generative edits for health ads by default, because rewritten copy can introduce claims the seller never approved ([PPC Land](https://ppc.land/metas-ai-advertising-gamble-brand-control-versus-algorithmic-efficiency/)).

**Nigerian tone: mostly hypotheses.** No source measured Pidgin versus English, pay-on-delivery lift or naira price formats. What exists is context: unknown social sellers are distrusted, and many small Nigerian sellers moved to WhatsApp for trust ([Techpoint Africa, 2019](https://techpoint.africa/2019/09/23/whatsapp-not-instagram-is-the-preferable-platform-for-small-businesses-in-nigeria/)). Sensible defaults to A/B test, not facts: plain English first, Pidgin as a variant; show the full naira price and delivery cost; list delivery areas, pay-on-delivery availability and a real WhatsApp number; use urgency only when true. The bot must ask the seller for every trust signal and never invent one.

**Production cost per asset (platform cost, estimated; re-price before setting credits).** Image generation APIs cost about $0.01-$0.20 per image: Gemini image about $0.039 ([BenchLM](https://benchlm.ai/md/media-pricing/nano-banana.md)), OpenAI gpt-image-1.5 $0.013 to $0.20 by quality ([OpenAI](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/models/gpt-image-1.5)), Ideogram about $0.03-$0.10 and strongest at text in images ([Puter](https://developer.puter.com/tutorials/ideogram-api-pricing/)), and Photoroom's AI-background tier about $0.10 ([Photoroom](https://www.photoroom.com/blog/remove-bg-api-cost-leonardo-ai-vs-photoroom)). The sound design is a hybrid: cut out the seller's real product, have AI generate only the background scene, and draw all text and prices with code so the wording and label stay exact. Budgeting three or four attempts per delivered asset puts a finished image set at roughly **$0.15-$0.40**. Fully generated video runs per second, from about $0.05 to $0.40: Veo 3.1 from $0.05 to $0.40 ([BenchLM](https://benchlm.ai/md/media-pricing/veo.md)), Kling 2.5 Turbo Pro $0.07 ([fal](https://fal.ai/learn/tools/ai-video-generators)), Runway $0.05-$0.12 ([Creatify](https://creatify.ai/blog/runway-pricing-(2026)-plans-credits-and-what-you-ll-actually-pay)). That implies about $1.50-$5 for a 15-second ad after retries. Template video built from the seller's real photos and clips with captions, voiceover and a price card costs far less: Remotion's official example shows about $0.02 for a one-minute simple render ([Remotion](https://remotion.dev/docs/lambda/cost-example)). Add voiceover at about $0.05-$0.10 per 1,000 characters on ElevenLabs ([AIReiter](https://aireiter.com/blog/elevenlabs-eleven-v3-api-guide)), so a 30-second script costs a few cents. My all-in estimates: **template video about $0.10-$0.30**, and a video with one short AI hero clip about **$1-$3**. Avatar tools such as HeyGen cost roughly $1.50-$20 per minute and carry the highest policy risk ([HeyGen](https://www.heygen.com/api)); skip them at first.

**AI video fails on exactly the thing you sell.** Vendor guides agree that generated video warps product shape, flickers logos and blurs label text ([Dreamina](https://dreamina.capcut.com/ai-video/ai-video-ecommerce)). The honest workflow keeps the real product pixels, uses AI only for lighting and background, and composites the label back. Voice coverage for Nigeria is unproven. ElevenLabs lists Hausa and Swahili on v3, with Igbo on the v4 list, but Yoruba and Nigerian Pidgin were not confirmed and no Nigerian-accent voice was tested ([ElevenLabs](https://elevenlabs.io/docs/help-center/other/what-languages-do-you-support)); test with Nigerian listeners before promising this feature. Honest limits: the AI cannot supply real reviews, real results or real people. Those must come from the seller with permission.

## Campaign strategy and the competitor gap

**Default build: one campaign, one ad set, 4-6 different creatives, and click-to-WhatsApp.** Practitioners advise consolidating small budgets and optimizing for the deepest event that can fire often enough. The documented learning threshold is about 50 optimization events per week per ad set ([Meta Developers](https://developers.meta.com/horizon/resources/launch-ad-campaign/)). Click-to-WhatsApp supports the Engagement, Leads, Sales and Traffic objectives and needs a linked Facebook Page ([Meta docs](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/marketing-api/ad-creative/messaging-ads/click-to-whatsapp)). Meta's own experiment is cited as showing 24% lower cost per lead when optimizing for leads rather than conversations ([WhatsApp Business](https://whatsappbusiness.com/resources/resource-library/lead-generation-strategies-smb-whatsapp/)). The math matters for your users: if a seller can afford ₦3,000 per order, 50 events a week is ₦150,000 a week, which most small sellers cannot fund (my arithmetic). So the bot should tell sellers plainly when their budget implies fewer than ~10 expected events a week, optimize for a cheaper upstream event, and judge success by orders confirmed in your database. Nigerian guides suggest ₦3,000-₦5,000 per day for tests and narrowing to the neighborhoods where the seller actually delivers ([7hubent](https://www.7hubent.com.ng/2025/10/how-to-run-profitable-facebook-ads-in.html), [Silicon Africa](https://siliconafrica.org/how-to-run-facebook-ads-for-small-businesses-in-nigeria/)); that conflicts with the global "go broad" view, and no independent test settles it. Set delivery areas by the seller's real coverage.

**Costs are cheap but poorly measured.** Global medians are click-through about 2.2-2.4% and cost per thousand impressions about $14-15 ([Triple Whale](https://www.triplewhale.com/blog/facebook-ads-benchmarks)). Nigeria shows about $1.50 CPM and $0.12 CPC, Kenya about $2.40 and $0.22, South Africa about $4.20 and $0.65, but these are 2026 projections, not measurements ([Adamigo](https://www.adamigo.ai/blog/meta-ads-cpm-cpc-benchmarks-by-country-2026)). No Ghana, health-category or cash-on-delivery return-rate data was found. Use these only as rough expectations and build your own benchmarks from real users.

**Tracking is your real moat.** For WhatsApp ads, Meta passes a click ID (`ctwa_clid`) on the first inbound message; storing it and sending later events back through the Conversions API lets Meta optimize toward real leads and purchases ([Whapi](https://whapi.cloud/blog/track-click-to-whatsapp-ctwa-clid), vendor docs; verify against Meta). For funnel pages, use the Pixel plus server events with a shared `event_id` so Meta can deduplicate ([Meta docs](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/ads-commerce/conversions-api/parameters/server-event)). Because your SaaS owns the order database, it can push confirmed and delivered orders back to Meta, including cash-on-delivery outcomes that Meta cannot see. Attribution windows also changed: from 12 January 2026 the Insights API no longer returns 7-day and 28-day view windows ([Meta Developers](https://developers.facebook.com/blog/post/2025/10/16/ads-insights-api-metric-availability-updates/)).

**Keeping ads running: proposed rules, all heuristics.** Wait at least 72 hours or about 1,000-2,000 impressions before judging an ad; pause ads that spend 2-3 times the target cost with no result; raise budgets at most 20% per step every 2-3 days, with seller approval for any increase ([Atria](https://www.tryatria.com/blog/how-to-scale-facebook-ads-profitably)). These are practitioner rules of thumb, not Meta rules, and 20% of a tiny budget is meaningless. Reported Meta rate limits for budget changes are unverified ([Porter Metrics](https://portermetrics.com/en/tutorial/meta-ads-mcp-ban/)).

**Competitors leave a gap.** Madgicx (from about $44-55 a month) and Birch/Revealbot optimize but do not create ads; AdCreative.ai (reported tiers $39/$249/$999) and Predis create but do not run; Zeely launches Meta campaigns for solo sellers with a reported 12% spend fee ([Toolradar](https://toolradar.com/compare/madgicx-vs-adcreative-ai), [Hackceleration](https://hackceleration.com/labs/compare/adcreativeai-vs-revealbot), [VidMuse](https://vidmuse.ai/blog/zeely-ai-review)). The notes found no African-built AI ad launcher and no naira pricing among global tools, and Nigerian ad help comes mostly from courses, freelancers and agencies. Meta itself aims to automate ad creation from a product image and a budget goal ([Exchange4media](https://www.exchange4media.com/digital-news/meta-aims-to-fully-automate-advertising-with-ai-by-2026-144037.html)), so the lasting advantage is not ad creation. It is the combination of naira pricing, WhatsApp and cash-on-delivery workflows, compliance gates and the order-database feedback loop. This gap is an inference from limited searches.

Architecture on your stack: Vercel API routes should enqueue render and publish jobs in Supabase and return immediately. Long video and image work should run on a worker, since Vercel functions are poorly suited to long FFmpeg or browser renders (general engineering knowledge). Hide every AI provider behind one interface, because prices moved several times in 2026. Store tokens encrypted. Use each seller's own authorization through your reviewed app, never shared tokens. Pace API writes and add backoff.

## What is solid, what is shaky, what to re-check

**Reasonably solid (multiple sources, or Meta text surfaced in search):**
- Third-party apps can create the full ad structure through Marketing API v25/v26, and legacy Advantage+ Shopping creation is closed.
- Real customers require Advanced Access to `ads_management`, which means App Review and Business Verification.
- Meta bills the ad account owner; Nigerian naira-card declines for Meta ads are widely reported, and VAT is added on top.
- Meta bans personal-attribute assertions and unsafe supplements, and restricts weight-loss ads and before-and-after images.
- Nigerian regulators (NAFDAC) require registration and ad clearance for drugs and herbal products.
- Image generation costs cents per image; template video costs cents per clip; fully generated video costs dollars and distorts product labels.

**Plausible but secondary (blogs, vendors, practitioners):**
- The May 2026 tier rename and the 500-call threshold (Meta blog surfaced, page not opened).
- 3-5 diverse concepts as the right creative count under Andromeda.
- Vertical safe-zone numbers and the reported performance lifts.
- Bank limit changes in Nigeria for 2026, and virtual-card fees.
- Competitor prices and the Zeely fee.
- The 20% budget step, the 72-hour wait and the other optimization thresholds.

**Unverified or contradictory (do not hard-code):**
- Whether a Nigeria-registered company can pass Business Verification, and what App Review currently asks.
- The exact permissions allowed inside a Login for Business configuration for customer-owned ad accounts, and whether the system-user flow is restricted to Tech Providers.
- Reseller, markup and credit-line rules, and whether any partner program is open to you.
- Current VAT rates (the sources are 2021-2023), ad account currency rules, and Meta's spending-limit and billing-threshold rules.
- Exact legacy-campaign shutdown dates, and whether the Africa-specific API restrictions are nil (none found).
- Aggregated Event Measurement status in 2026, and API creation of pixels for customer accounts.
- The Nigeria and Kenya benchmark figures (projections), the Creative Similarity 60% claim, the 12-79% CTWA cost gains (single vendor), and the 7-day free messaging window.
- Yoruba and Nigerian Pidgin voice support, music licensing, and AI-disclosure rules for realistic synthetic people.
- Pricing and commercial-use terms for each image, video and voice provider; the notes used aggregator pages.

**Must be re-checked against Meta's live documentation before building (owner: your lead developer plus a lawyer):**
1. App Dashboard: the actual App Review checklist, Business Verification requirements for Nigeria, privacy-policy and data-deletion rules, and which permissions can be requested.
2. Marketing API reference for v26: endpoints for ad images, videos, custom audiences, targeting search and pixel creation; click-to-WhatsApp `destination_type` and `promoted_object` values; the removed forecasting fields.
3. Meta Business Help Center: payment methods and billing thresholds in Nigeria, ad-account currency rules, and spending limits.
4. Meta Platform Terms and Commercial Terms: automation limits, rebilling and resale, partner eligibility.
5. Advertising Standards pages (health, personal attributes, misleading claims, AI disclosure, special categories): read the full text and note the page dates.
6. Rate limits and Advantage+ campaign minimums.
7. Nigerian counsel on NAFDAC ad approval for food supplements, FCCPC rules on misleading ads and MLM, NDPA direct-marketing consent, and any CBN licensing exposure from holding customer funds.
8. Live pricing pages for every AI provider you plan to use, and their commercial-use and AI-label (C2PA) behavior.
9. A pilot with real Nigerian accounts to learn actual rejection and restriction behavior, because no source measured it.

## Conclusion

The question changes shape once the evidence is in: "can a bot run ads?" is answered yes, but the harder questions are whether Meta will approve you, whether your sellers can pay Meta, and whether the bot can keep health sellers inside the rules. The strongest product is therefore not a hands-off autopilot. It is a compliance-first assistant that drafts honest, varied ads, refuses risky claims, publishes only with approval, and feeds real order outcomes back to Meta, which generic tools in this research did not offer.

Two early moves reduce risk the most: start the Business Verification and legal review now, because those run on others' calendars, and run a small pilot with real Nigerian accounts to measure payment failure and ad rejection, which no source quantified.

# Technical appendix

## A1. Marketing API build sequence (verify each step on v26)

Order from Meta's click-to-WhatsApp guide: campaign, ad set, ad creative, ad. Use `POST /act_<ID>/campaigns` with a messaging-compatible objective (`OUTCOME_ENGAGEMENT`, `OUTCOME_LEADS`, `OUTCOME_SALES` or `OUTCOME_TRAFFIC`) and `special_ad_categories=[]` only after the seller confirms none apply ([Meta](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/marketing-api/ad-creative/messaging-ads/click-to-whatsapp)). Creatives use `object_story_spec` with `link_data`, and when `object_story_spec` is supplied, `asset_feed_spec` is ignored ([Meta](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/ads-commerce/marketing-api/call-ads)). Image upload returns an `image_hash`. These endpoints (`/adimages`, `/advideos`, `/customaudiences`, `/targetingsearch`, `/adsets`, `/ads`, `/delivery_estimate`) come from general knowledge and were **not confirmed** in the research. Read `advantage_state_info` to confirm Advantage+ qualification (single secondary source). For housing, employment and financial ad sets, v26.0 requires an explicit `targeting_automation.advantage_audience` value or creation fails ([ppc.land](https://ppc.land/meta-blocks-47-commerce-endpoints-as-graph-api-v26-0-lands-today/)). v26.0 also removed three delivery-forecasting fields with no successor (secondary), the Instagram Explore placement and the `story` value in `messenger_positions` ([unalsoft](https://unalsoft.com/blog/2026-07-31-meta-graph-api-v26/en/)). Pin an explicit API version and schedule a version bump about every six months.

## A2. Auth, tokens, limits

- Use Facebook Login for Business with a `config_id`. Permissions are set in the configuration instead of a runtime `scope` (GitHub examples, not spec).
- Long-lived user tokens last about 60 days; system-user tokens may be non-expiring or 60-day. Encrypt in Supabase, never log, and detect invalidation with a "reconnect" state.
- Rate limits are per ad account per use case (Business Use Case). Reads cost about 1 point and writes about 3; the development tier maxes at a score of 60 with a 300-second decay; throttling returns error 613, subcode 1487742 ([Meta](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/ads-commerce/marketing-api/overview/rate-limiting)). The per-hour formula with active-ad multipliers is third-party and pre-rename.
- Promotion to Full tier: 500 calls in 15 days, under 15% errors over the last 500 calls ([Meta blog](https://developers.meta.com/blog/updates-to-ads-management-standard-access-feature/)).

## A3. Tracking implementation

- Browser: `fbq('track','Lead',{}, {eventID})`. Server: `POST /{dataset_id}/events` from a Next.js Route Handler with the same `event_id` and `event_name`, hashed `em`/`ph`, `fbp`/`fbc`, `client_user_agent`, `event_source_url`. Meta keeps the first matching event within 48 hours ([Meta](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/ads-commerce/conversions-api/parameters/server-event)). Events older than 7 days risk failing the batch (third party, [adsuploader](https://adsuploader.com/blog/meta-conversions-api)).
- WhatsApp: capture `ctwa_clid` from the referral object on the first inbound message through the WhatsApp Business Platform webhook, link it to the order, then send CAPI events with `action_source=business_messaging`. Whether sellers using the plain WhatsApp Business app (not the Platform API) can support this is **unverified**.
- Aggregated Event Measurement status and domain-verification needs for custom funnel domains in 2026: unconfirmed.
- Insights: pull spend, impressions, reach, frequency, clicks, inline link clicks, CTR, CPM, actions and cost per action, with breakdowns by ad, placement, device, region and hour; use `1d_click` and `7d_click`. Hourly and unique-count breakdowns keep only 13 months of history ([Supermetrics](https://docs.supermetrics.com/docs/facebook-ads-new-historical-limitations-attribution-window-and-metric-removals-january-12-2026)).

## A4. Creative pipeline on Vercel + Supabase

1. API route inserts a `render_jobs` row and enqueues (pgmq, Inngest or Vercel's own queue; verify) and returns at once.
2. Image: background removal and cutout of the real product, AI scene generation (Photoroom Plus, Gemini, FLUX Kontext or Ideogram via a gateway such as fal.ai), then deterministic layout with Satori/HTML and Sharp for 1:1, 4:5 and 9:16, keeping key content inside safe zones. Satori on Vercel is fast but supports only a CSS subset (general knowledge).
3. Video: Remotion Lambda (webhook on completion) or a small container worker running FFmpeg for trim, 9:16 reframing, kinetic captions, hook text, music ducking and loudness normalization. Remotion licensing for companies of four or more people needs checking ([Spotsaas, third party](https://www.spotsaas.com/blog/remotion-review)).
4. Optional AI hero clip (3-5 seconds, image-to-video) with the real label re-composited and a first/middle/last frame QA check.
5. Voice and captions: Claude writes scripts from seller-supplied facts only; ElevenLabs for TTS; a Whisper-class transcriber for captions with seller edit.
6. Output to Supabase Storage; realtime updates to the UI; log provenance (model, prompt, source photo) and keep C2PA metadata intact. Music licensing source: **not researched**.

## A5. Compliance engine (pre-publish checks, in order)

1. Claim scan (rules plus an LLM classifier): disease, cure, treat, prevent, guarantee, quantified results, unsafe ingredients.
2. Second-person attribute detector; body-shaming and fake-urgency detector.
3. Income and recruitment detector (amounts with earn/weekly, "passive income", "join my team").
4. Image checks, including OCR of text inside images through the same filters: no before-and-after, body close-ups, scales, earnings screenshots, Meta logos or synthetic people.
5. Landing-page fetch and rescan; ad and page must match.
6. Targeting check: adults only for health products; no sensitive-attribute targeting; special-category stop.
7. Regulatory gate: NAFDAC number captured per product; advert approval number for drugs and herbals; country rules.
8. Velocity and near-duplicate limiter per ad account; draft-first; no auto-resubmit; pause on disapproval or Account Quality warning.
9. Logged consent and a plain responsibility notice to the seller.

Test the checker against real Meta review outcomes; it was not validated in this research.
