Two businesses. One platform. More opportunities to grow.

MAXIMUS is a business community that helps people learn how to build two types of businesses: an online business and an offline product-based business.

2×

The idea is simple

We don't want people to limit themselves to just one way of doing business. We introduce them to both the digital business world and the traditional product business world.

2 · The offline business

Learn the products. Build customers & a team.

This is where we introduce people to product-based network marketing.

You learn about the products, understand their benefits and how they are used, introduce them to potential customers and develop relationships with people who may want to buy them.

As you develop, you can also introduce other people to the business, help them understand how it works and build a team.

You earn according to qualifying product sales and the company's compensation structure. That is how the offline business works.
