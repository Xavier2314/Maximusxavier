Who we are

A community of people building businesses.

MAXIMUS is a business community. We help people learn how to build two kinds of business: an online business using digital skills, and an offline business selling products. See how both work.

You don't need business experience to start. What you get here is a place to learn, people who've done it before, and a team that pushes each other to keep going.
