Privacy policy.

This policy describes how the website handles information. Please have it reviewed for your jurisdiction before launch and add your official contact details.

Information we collect

We only collect information you choose to give us — for example when you send a message through the contact form, register for an event or subscribe to the MAXIMUS Letter. This may include your name, email address, phone number and the content of your message.

How we use it

  • To respond to your enquiries and messages.
  • To manage event registrations you request.
  • To send newsletters and announcements you have subscribed to.
  • To improve the website and our community activities.

What we don't do

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers that help us operate the website and communications (such as form or email providers), and only for those purposes.

Cookies & storage

This website uses minimal browser storage to remember interface preferences, such as whether the intro animation has already played during your visit. It does not use advertising cookies.

Third-party content

Some images are served from third-party providers. Links to external websites or social platforms are governed by those platforms' own privacy policies.

Your choices

You can unsubscribe from emails at any time using the link in any message, and you may ask us to access, correct or delete the information you have shared with us by contacting us through the Contact page.

Updates

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available on this page.
