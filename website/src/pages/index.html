Frequently asked

Questions, answered.

Still curious? Read MAXIMUS in 2 minutes or talk to us.

What is MAXIMUS, in one sentence?

MAXIMUS is a business community that helps people learn how to build two types of businesses: an online business using digital skills, and an offline product-based business.

What is the online business?

You learn a digital skill (like website design, digital marketing, email marketing, graphic design or social media management), find people who need it, offer your service and get paid for the work you do.

What is the offline business?

It's product-based network marketing. You learn about the products and their benefits, introduce them to customers and build relationships. As you grow, you can introduce others to the business and build a team. You earn according to qualifying product sales and the company's compensation structure.

Do I have to do both?

No. You can start with the one that suits you. The two work well together, because the skills you build in one (selling, communicating, looking after clients) help you in the other.

Do I need experience to start?

No. You don't have to know everything before you start. You learn, practise and develop as you go, with training, mentorship and support from the community.

Is income guaranteed?

No. MAXIMUS teaches skills and business models, but it doesn't promise income. What you earn depends on your own effort, the work and sales you make, and, for the offline business, the company's compensation structure.

How do I join?

Send us a message on the Contact page. Tell us a little about yourself and your goals, and someone from the team will explain both pathways and your next steps.
