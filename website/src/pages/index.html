Frequently asked

Questions, answered.

Still curious? Talk to us.

What is MAXIMUS?

MAXIMUS is a personal growth, freelancing, entrepreneurship and career-development community. It provides an environment where young, driven individuals develop their skills, embrace independence and work towards meaningful careers.

Who is MAXIMUS for?

Ambitious individuals who want to grow — aspiring freelancers, early-career professionals, students, creatives and would-be entrepreneurs who are ready to take ownership of their development.

Do I need experience to join?

No. What matters most is a willingness to learn, commitment and consistent action. MAXIMUS is built for people at different stages of their journey.

Does MAXIMUS guarantee income or a job?

No. MAXIMUS does not promise income, employment or specific career outcomes. We focus on mindset, skills, structure and community — the results each person achieves depend on their own effort and circumstances.

Are the programmes certified qualifications?

MAXIMUS focus areas are developmental, not formal certifications. Any specific programme, and what it includes, is described clearly when it is published.

How do I hear about events?

Verified upcoming events are listed on the Events page. You can also subscribe to the MAXIMUS Letter in the footer to receive announcements.

How do I get involved?

Send a community enquiry through the Contact page and tell us a little about yourself and your goals. The team will follow up with next steps.
