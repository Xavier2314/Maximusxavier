# Script 02 — Client Landing Page Project: Requirements Gathering & Solution Proposal

**Purpose:** Train team members on scoping a landing page or website project, managing client expectations, and proposing the right platform.  
**Skill Focus:** Requirements gathering, platform recommendation, scope definition, handling "can you just..." requests.

---

## Characters

- **Jordan** — Freelancer (you)
- **Derek** — Client, a business coach launching a new online course

---

## Scene: Discovery Call — Landing Page Project

---

**Jordan:** Hey Derek, good to connect. I saw on your intake form that you need a landing page built for a new course launch. Before we get into the build, I want to make sure I understand exactly what you need so I can recommend the right approach. Sound good?

**Derek:** Yeah, absolutely. I've been going back and forth on this for weeks and I just need someone to tell me what to do honestly.

**Jordan:** I appreciate that, and I'll do exactly that. Let's start with the basics — do you already have a website, and if so, what's it built on?

**Derek:** I have a Wix site. It's pretty basic, just my about page and a contact form.

**Jordan:** Okay, good. So we have two options — we can build the landing page directly inside your existing Wix site, or we can build it on a separate platform like ClickFunnels or Systeme.io if you need more advanced funnel features. Do you know yet what you want visitors to do when they land on the page? Is this a sales page where they buy directly, or are you capturing emails first and selling later?

**Derek:** I want them to sign up for a free webinar first. Then on the thank-you page, there's a low-ticket offer — like a $47 workbook.

**Jordan:** Perfect, that's a two-step funnel. So page one captures the email and registers them for the webinar, and page two offers the $47 product. Are you running this webinar live or is it an automated replay?

**Derek:** I'm doing it live first, then I want to automate it later.

**Jordan:** Got it. What platform are you hosting the webinar on — Zoom, WebinarJam, something else?

**Derek:** Zoom right now.

**Jordan:** Okay. And for collecting payments on the $47 offer — do you have a payment processor set up? Stripe, PayPal?

**Derek:** I have Stripe but I've never connected it to anything.

**Jordan:** That's fine, we can handle that. Last piece — where are your email subscribers going? Do you have an email marketing platform like Mailchimp, ConvertKit, ActiveCampaign?

**Derek:** I use Mailchimp but I only have like 200 subscribers. I'm not sure if I should switch.

**Jordan:** You don't need to switch for this project. Mailchimp will work fine. Alright, let me summarize what I'm building a picture of. You need: a registration page connected to Zoom, an email opt-in that feeds into Mailchimp, a thank-you page with a Stripe payment link for the $47 workbook, and you want all of this to live somewhere that's easy for you to manage. Is that right?

**Derek:** Yes. And I want it to look professional — I've seen some landing pages that look really cheap.

**Jordan:** Understood. Design quality matters and we'll make sure it matches your brand. I do want to be honest with you about one thing — Wix has some limitations when it comes to advanced funnel features and third-party integrations. For a two-step funnel with Stripe and Mailchimp both connecting cleanly, I'd actually recommend we build this outside of Wix using **Systeme.io** or we keep it in Wix but use a workaround with **Zapier** to connect the pieces. Systeme.io is free up to a point and built exactly for this type of funnel. Would you be open to that?

**Derek:** I mean, I don't want to have to manage another platform.

**Jordan:** That's a fair concern. Here's my honest take: Systeme.io is actually *less* complex to manage than wiring together Wix + Zapier + Mailchimp + Stripe manually. Once it's built, your funnel lives in one place. But if you feel strongly about staying in Wix, we can make it work — it just adds a bit of complexity and cost to the build. I want you to make that call with full information.

**Derek:** Okay, when you put it that way, I'm open to Systeme.io. Can you walk me through what I'd be paying for that?

**Jordan:** Systeme.io's free plan covers up to 2,000 contacts and unlimited funnels, so you likely won't need to pay them anything for a while. My fee for the build would be in the range of **$600 to $900** — that covers the two-page funnel design and build, Stripe integration, Mailchimp connection, Zoom registration link, and one round of revisions. I'd also write basic copy outlines if you don't have copy ready, but final copy is your responsibility or we can scope that as an add-on.

**Derek:** How long does it take?

**Jordan:** For this scope, **5 to 7 business days** from the moment I have all your assets — logo, headshot, any brand colors or fonts, and your course details. The biggest delay I typically see is waiting on clients for those materials.

**Derek:** Can you just build it fast and I'll fill in the copy later?

**Jordan:** I can build the structure first and drop in placeholder text, but I want to set the expectation clearly: I can't do final QA or hand it off until the real copy is in, because copy affects layout. So "placeholder first, fill in later" is fine, but it adds a revision round on your end. Just want you to know that upfront so there's no surprise at the end.

**Derek:** Fair enough. That makes sense. What do you need from me to get started?

**Jordan:** I'll send you a short onboarding form today — it asks for your brand assets, your Zoom link once it's created, your Stripe account email, and your Mailchimp API key. Once I have those back, I can start within 24 hours. I also take a 50% deposit upfront before starting work.

**Derek:** Works for me. Send it over.

**Jordan:** Will do. One last question — do you have a launch date in mind?

**Derek:** I want to go live in three weeks.

**Jordan:** With a 7-day build and your materials coming back quickly, that's very doable. I'll note that in the proposal so we're aligned on the timeline.

**Derek:** Perfect. Thanks, Jordan.

**Jordan:** Thank you, Derek. Talk soon.

## Debrief Notes for Trainees

| Skill | What to Notice |
|---|---|
| **Platform expertise** | Jordan doesn't just accept the client's tool choice — he evaluates it and recommends better options with clear reasoning |
| **Handling pushback** | When Derek resists changing platforms, Jordan doesn't cave or argue — he explains the tradeoff and lets Derek decide |
| **Scope discipline** | Jordan defines what's included AND what's not (copy) to prevent scope creep |
| **The "can you just..." request** | Jordan says yes to the underlying need but explains the consequence — this is the professional way to handle it |
| **Onboarding logistics** | Jordan ends with a clear next step and a deposit requirement — not vague "let's move forward" language |

---

## Practice Exercise

Role-play this script. The client should ask "Can you also set up my email sequences in Mailchimp?" mid-call. Practice acknowledging the request, defining it as a separate scope item, and offering to add it as an optional line in the proposal without derailing the current conversation.
