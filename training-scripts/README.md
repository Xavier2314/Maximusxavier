## How to Use These Scripts

### For Self-Study
Read through each script alone, paying attention to the **Debrief Notes** section at the end of each one. These highlight the key skills being demonstrated.

### For Pair Practice
1. One person plays the Freelancer, one plays the Client
2. The Client should try to go off-script once or twice to test adaptability
3. After the role-play, use the Debrief Notes as a checklist — did the Freelancer cover each skill?

### For Team Training Sessions
Assign scripts as pre-reading before the session. During the session, role-play only the most challenging scene from each script (usually the objection or negotiation moment), then debrief as a group.

### Recommended Progression

**Week 1 — Foundation:** Scripts 01 and 02 (basic discovery and requirements gathering)  
**Week 2 — Technical Depth:** Scripts 03 and 04 (integration-specific discovery)  
**Week 3 — Complex Scenarios:** Scripts 05 and 08 (multi-client architecture and outreach)  
**Week 4 — Business Skills:** Scripts 06 and 07 (pricing, scope, delivery, and payment)

## Key Principles Across All Scripts

1. **Ask before proposing** — never jump to a solution until you've fully mapped the problem
2. **Reflect back** — always restate your understanding before pricing or scoping
3. **Specificity builds trust** — vague answers lose clients; concrete numbers and timelines win them
4. **Price is not negotiable by default** — know your floor and explain the value, don't just discount
5. **Scope adds-ons get written approval** — never start work on unscoped requests without documentation
6. **Test live on the call** — don't tell clients it works; show them
7. **Follow up** — most freelancers disappear after delivery; the ones who follow up get referrals
